# Observability and resource controls

Collect enough local evidence to diagnose the owner's actual failures; no separate telemetry platform or mandatory security dashboard is required. Apply the [personal-tool amendment](../changes/2026-10-10-personal-tool-revision.md) and [directory-results amendment](../changes/2026-10-10-directory-results-revision.md).

## Logs

Structured logs include timestamp, severity, component, request/job/operation ID, library/profile identity (redacted where exported), stage, outcome and duration. Exclude owner/agent/provider tokens, content-delivery credentials, comparison/polling secrets, question bodies, raw source, HTML, note text, absolute approved roots and sensitive document titles by default. Error reports sanitize hostile content and support a deliberate redacted diagnostic export. If a delivery credential appears in a URL path or hostname, redact that component before request/proxy diagnostics are stored; logging a full delivery URL is credential logging. Correlate through a nonsecret delivery-session/transfer identity instead. Distinguish an observed explicit close, lost renewal/lease expiry, current owner-access rejection and authorized recreation under the [delivery authority contract](../contracts/authentication.md#content-delivery-authority); do not log an unobserved crash or mobile suspension as confirmed abandonment.

## Metrics and health

Expose relevant local diagnostics for active/queued/paused jobs, provider attempt counts, uncertain/orphan attempts, known usage, current attempt_generation and cancellation-to-dispatch decisions, structural/semantic coverage, import/validation failures, DB busy/transaction latency, authenticated event connections/replays, stream_epoch changes/resyncs, receipt replays/fingerprint conflicts, active source/read/staging/backup pins, session rejections and content-store size. Include available free space for live/staging/backup destinations and actual persisted staging bytes, with unavailable measurements stated honestly. Authenticated `GET /uploads` discovery and per-upload status expose pending upload identity/state and accepted progress for resume or explicit abandonment. Separate total staged bytes from currently reclaimable bytes after current adoption and persisted transfer/job/recovery pin checks; an unadopted context is not automatically reclaimable. Expose the existing backup status: last complete checksum-verified set/time and its age, incomplete work and latest safe failure reason; distinguish the last actual trial-restore result from checksum verification. Counts exposed through UI remain owner-authenticated and lifecycle-aware. Public liveness returns no project/content information. Readiness distinguishes DB recovery, content failure and provider-unavailable status; an unavailable provider is not necessarily an unreadable library.

## Work budgets and performance experiments

Use the owner-configurable work budgets in [configuration](../contracts/configuration.md) for AI jobs. Default semantic worker concurrency is 1, not one worker per document. Request-count/deadline budgets are application-enforceable even when subscription token/quota telemetry is unavailable. A budget pause records incomplete coverage and a clear reason, not a fabricated fully scanned result. These job controls do not impose an HTML/file/package size, file-count or directory-depth limit.

Benchmark metadata/search using the recorded host and fixture corpus specified in [requirements](../requirements.md). Report p50/p95, directory/page/file shape and actual workload sizes. Measure first-page loading, requested asset bytes and trusted-host responsiveness separately, including module/CDN failure and close/reload or app-restart recovery. Ordinary networking is allowed; external-resource failure is a dependency failure. Provider/network latency is not part of the local metadata target.

Record streamed/chunked bytes, completed versus pending files, cursor progress, resumed chunks, range reads, hashing/indexing work and cancellation. X18/X20 workloads above the former package/file-count/context thresholds must preserve complete selected content without a full-directory in-memory envelope. Processing batch sizes are implementation details, never total-content quotas. Actual out-of-space, I/O, memory, browser or provider failures identify the affected step and recovery path; do not silently truncate, skip files or publish incomplete versions.

### Storage backpressure and cleanup

Before accepting more staged bytes, account for the destination's actual ability to continue the operation. If space or I/O prevents safe progress, pause/fail the affected write with its accepted position, reason and resume/cancel/cleanup path. Continue serving available verified reads when the service can do so safely. Internal reserves or flow-control thresholds must be documented as resource/recovery mechanisms, not a fixed total artifact/context admission ceiling or permission to drop files.

Reclaim only eligible abandoned/unadopted staging under the [backup/recovery](backup-recovery.md) and [viewer transfer](../contracts/viewer-protocol.md#large-context-transfer-messages) lifetime rules. Current adoption and persisted transfer/job/copy/backup/recovery pins take precedence. Delivery lease expiry, lost heartbeat or mobile background suspension alone never authorizes upload cleanup. Record the cleanup result and remaining space; do not require creating a new full backup before freeing otherwise eligible data. Diagnostic cleanup follows configured retention and preserves essential receipts/audit state.

## Event and health UX

UI displays Connected, Reconnecting, Session expired, Source unavailable and Provider unavailable as distinct states. Missing browser storage returns to Connect with a storage-loss explanation; it is not automatically a server-session expiry or service outage. Delivery renewal/recreation with a still-valid owner session shows recovery of the selected content instead of an unnecessary pairing screen. Preserve pending upload visibility while a page or phone is suspended. Backup visibility distinguishes not yet created, complete, incomplete/interrupted and failed attempts, without treating an unconfigured schedule as an operational failure. Event updates carry resource revisions and stream_epoch/sequence; clients refetch confirmed state on gaps or changed epoch. A receipt proves an operation outcome, not the current resource revision, and stale-cache mutation is not acknowledged. Job status distinguishes completed-with-incomplete-coverage from failed, and cancelling from cancelled. Every report states assessed target and last actual check time.

## Operations evidence

No real credentials or private source are included in logs/artifacts committed to a repository. Acceptance evidence uses small focused fixtures and redacted output. Exercise space failure, interrupted staging, discoverable actual staged bytes after restart, cleanup racing with context adoption, a failed backup replacement and renderer/download closure or lease recovery separately from owner-access termination; verify the displayed failure/recovery state without recording private payloads or credentials. Store enough request/job correlation to investigate crashes without retaining private model reasoning. Retention of diagnostics cannot destroy unsettled operation receipts or essential audit decisions.

## Quality evidence and observation limits

Record evaluation results under [knowledge quality](../testing/knowledge-quality.md): declared claims, resolved/unavailable citations, semantic support/contradictions/uncertainty, retrieval misses, reader task outcomes and actual coverage. Keep this evidence separate from operational liveness and latency. Provider confidence, a Reviewed badge or a structurally complete scan is not an accuracy metric.

A source observation includes comparison base/target and inspected paths, with indirect scope limits. Dashboards must not turn unchanged direct evidence or partial semantic work into all-current status. Usage/budget reports retain uncertain attempts rather than counting only live local workers.

Reader participation and timing/reuse observations are explicit evaluation records, not silently enabled behavioral telemetry. Do not publish private questions, source bodies, annotations or identifiable browsing events as metrics. Preserve authorized diagnostic data under the owner's configured retention and retain required recovery/audit state.
