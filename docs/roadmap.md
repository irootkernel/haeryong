## Early evidence within sequential execution

EPIC-001 begins official provider/deployment eligibility checks in TASK-001-01. That task predeclares a concrete question and comparison. Its [initial worksheet](testing/knowledge-quality.md#initial-reader-comparison) records not_available because the selected final-report revisions and existing explanations are unbound; expected implementation facts and current friction remain unmeasured. This is preliminary evidence, not G-VALUE or an added product milestone. Stack, remembered private auth, useful JavaScript with separate host authority and a real exact-source provider question establish the narrow prerequisites. TASK-001-07 runs the real two-project understanding workflow after TASK-001-05 and before TASK-001-06 freezes current contracts. It is an isolated prototype inside the current Epic, not unrecorded parallel production work.

The [knowledge-quality method](testing/knowledge-quality.md) starts with a proposed 3–5 explanations and 20–30 material claims, including a Markdown/ADR comparison. The owner selected Mulgae/Haepari at their final reports after approved validation; exact committed revisions and source/disclosure scope must be bound before consumption. Citation resolution, semantic support, reader outcomes and observed effort remain separate. No percentage or sample count implies universal accuracy.

Reuse the real corpus at reader, links/search, notes/recovery, production AI, private remote and maintenance gates. TASK-003-06 checks import/open/interaction/reopen and original-directory download in browser/Wails; TASK-004-06 checks finding and reusing an existing explanation; TASK-005-06 checks real notes/Review and recovery; TASK-006-06 checks saved, cited production Q&A; TASK-007-06 repeats the complete workflow on a real private second device with Wails closed. Each checkpoint consumes only already delivered features. Record concrete failures and the next bounded repair; use the knowledge-quality reconsideration rule when that repair does not restore the expected reader outcome. TASK-010-05 is final acceptance regression, not the first opportunity to learn whether explanations are useful.

## Delivery boundaries

Stage A includes the connected reader, Haeryong-owned skill, supported real subscription AI, Wails and private authenticated second-device browsing. EPIC-007 closes it only after C01–C09, M08, M10, M14 and M15–M20 pass under their current owner-amended applicability on the selected environment and the knowledge-quality workflow passes. A desktop-only demonstration, static fallback, anonymous export or fake provider cannot satisfy the complete milestone.

Stage B adds manual semantic maintenance and configurable per-category disabled/propose/auto overlays with evidence, audit and safe undo. Meaning-changing publication and personal Review remain explicit. Stage C schedules that same validated work and adds owner-confirmed retention. These capabilities remain product commitments; narrower prototypes are not substitutes for them.

M08, M14 and M20 have their primary Stage A proof by TASK-007-06, drawing on the already delivered viewer/source/session/restore features. EPIC-010 references are release regression, not a prerequisite for closing EPIC-007.

EPIC-010 qualifies the complete release and selected distribution. Signing/public distribution steps depend on the actual release channel; install, data integrity, privacy and recovery evidence remain necessary for any relied-on installation.

## Planning and change rules

Use the [task index](tasks/README.md) and [execution protocol](execution-protocol.md). Task count is not fixed. G-STACK, G-AUTH, G-VIEW, G-AI, G-VALUE and G-CONTRACT must all pass for the chosen baseline; recording a failed gate is not passing it.

Freeze only evidence-backed current contract details and durable identities/provenance, with later surfaces explicitly provisional. Resolve a failing candidate through a bounded, recorded design correction. Preserve current owner requirements, billing choices, host/session separation and data integrity. Applying the already authorized D-08/D-09 directions does not reopen owner approval or add superseded security, single-page or artifact-quota gates. Update dependent examples, fixtures and task owners together.
