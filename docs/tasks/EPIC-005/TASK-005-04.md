## Delivered result

The owner purges a trashed document through the same exact impact plan as the other
lifecycle actions. The plan shows separate counts for versions, notes and their full
history, Review history, distinct referring documents and edges, and states honestly
that the last complete backup status is unavailable and that a new backup is not
required. Commit makes the document terminal, clears its title and latest pointer,
permanently reserves every old version ID and queues one cleanup job, all in one
transaction with a stable 202 receipt; a concurrent restore and purge select one
result, and purge cannot be cancelled or undone after acceptance. The worker deletes
the target's personal, derived, source and version records in foreign-key order and
collects content only after rechecking every version, draft, upload, backup, source,
read, job and staging owner immediately before collection. Shared and source bytes
survive, incoming references keep a minimal tombstone with no title or body, and old
plans and receipts keep their identity and comparison facts without private copies.
A recoverable error or a still-protected object pauses the same job, which resumes
automatically and completes only when every candidate is settled.

Purge plans, the 202 result, the honest backup line and the purge capability are
additive provisional contracts at schema revision 13, whose declaration checks chain
from revision 8. A purged version ID is rejected at import acceptance and final
publication. The browser, native and desktop clients share these operations, the Trash
screen shows the destructive confirmation and per-job progress, and the CLI adds
`lifecycle plan --action purge`.

  tests, and the desktop race and vet run.
- Focused checks passed on the candidates stated in the record: restore and purge
