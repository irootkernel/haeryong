## Delivered result

The owner archives, unarchives, trashes and restores a whole document through an
exact impact plan. The plan is bound to the document revision and lists the affected
versions, personal records and incoming references; it states each operation's
effect, expires after 15 minutes and is committed once with explicit confirmation.
Trash records the previous active or archived state and hides the document from normal
views, search, direct files, links and the shared eligible-input contract; incoming
references show as unavailable and new personal writes return 409 INVALID_TRANSITION.
Restore returns the same document, versions, references, Notes with their anchors and
history, Review and Revisit to that recorded state, without recreating or re-dispatching
anything. Trash cancels captured import and derived work, retires open renderer
deliveries and closes their frames; neither late results nor old deliveries revive
after restore, and a fresh navigation is required.

Plan create, GET and commit, the owner Trash list and the lifecycle capability are
additive provisional contracts at schema revision 12, whose declaration checks chain
from revision 8. Request validation precedes receipt replay; the plan ID is the natural
commit identity and an identical replay returns the original result even after expiry.
Automatic reading-position saves do not make an open plan stale. The browser, native
and desktop clients share these operations, the Reader More menu and Trash screen
present them, and the CLI adds `lifecycle plan`, `lifecycle show`, `lifecycle commit`
and `trash list`.
