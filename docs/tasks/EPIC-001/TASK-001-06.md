## Implementation work

1. Incorporate stack/auth/viewer/provider/value findings through bounded ADR changes without reopening owner directions by convenience.
2. Freeze the validated directory/version/page identity, optional provenance/reference semantics, html_js_v1 loader behavior and current API/config/error fields in the schema registry and its delegated import-workflow contract. Freeze purpose/target action authorization, explicit document_id, client pre-transfer versus API-omitted base, distinct preparation/final keys, summary/detail allocation and cancel/abandon coupling with concrete fixtures. Record D-10's actually tested private renderer mappings and configuration. Plain directory/ZIP/single-HTML intake, generated metadata, streamed/chunked transport and no arbitrary size/count/depth cap are the baseline. Keep unimplemented future features explicitly provisional; do not create unused tables merely to claim freeze completeness.
3. Ratify the initial hardware/corpus/workload, proposed latency/search/concurrency targets and the treatment of a missed target using the initial evidence. Record adopted values, diagnostic-only metrics, remaining limits and the owning feature checks; do not convert sample size or artifact workload into a product cap. Map every currently enabled UI action to its concrete read/mutation contract and keep later actions explicitly provisional.
4. Reconcile executable examples, task dependencies and fixture schemas. Require passing G-STACK, G-AUTH, G-VIEW, G-AI, G-VALUE and G-CONTRACT evidence before closing EPIC-001.
