# TASK-001-07: Validate the two-project understanding workflow

## Outcome

Pass G-VALUE with a bounded real-source prototype before freezing durable contracts. This is an early understanding-workflow experiment, not a claim that Stage A or full AI maintenance is implemented.

## Inputs

- [testing/knowledge-quality.md](../../testing/knowledge-quality.md)
- [testing/fixtures.md](../../testing/fixtures.md)
- [contracts/package.md](../../contracts/package.md)
- [contracts/viewer-protocol.md](../../contracts/viewer-protocol.md)
- [examples/use-haeryong-skill.md](../../examples/use-haeryong-skill.md)

## Environment prerequisites

- Two approved source projects and exact selected revisions, supported provider/disclosure scope and a named reader/evaluator; label agent-only evaluation and reuse the available G-AUTH/G-VIEW client and private-route harnesses.

## Implementation work

1. Select actual revisions from two owner-approved projects. Mulgae/Haepari retry and approval explanations are candidates only, not inspected source facts. Start with a proposed 3–5 explanations and 20–30 material claims, including a Markdown/ADR explanation; record the actual sample and selection rationale. Carry forward TASK-001-01's reader question and benefit_observed/no_difference/not_available observation; retain no_difference and not_available as predeclared risks with a concrete comparison or missing-input resolution. Declare each reader task's expected answer, evidence, success observable and blocking error before generation/evaluation.
2. Use one Haeryong-owned draft skill and the verified provider/source/viewer path to create and inspect result directories in an isolated prototype, including multiple linked HTML pages with nested assets and no compulsory hand-authored manifest. Reuse a prior exact-version/page/region across projects, follow its evidence, ask a source-cited question and create a meaningful JavaScript interaction using normal modules/frameworks or CDN resources where useful, and compare its explanatory value with a simple Markdown/ADR explanation.
3. Reuse the G-AUTH/G-VIEW harnesses rather than building the production reader or personal-state service. Where a small fixture already permits it, demonstrate v1/v2 note/Review/pinned-reference boundaries or a second-device reopen; record unsupported portions explicitly. These selected examples contribute to C07/M15 and other later scenarios but cannot pass their complete production criteria here.
4. Apply the knowledge-quality method to direct and indirect changes, an unrelated commit, historical scope, missing source/coverage, false thematic dependency, and pinned/rejected decisions. These are early quality experiments; production semantic maintenance still belongs to EPIC-008.
5. Record the selected reader's answers to concrete change/failure/impact questions and effort relative to the existing diff-plus-agent workflow. Recommend that the owner perform one suitable reader task; record actual participation and reuse observations, or label owner usefulness unmeasured under the [checkpoint rule](../../testing/knowledge-quality.md#progressive-use-checkpoints). This recommendation adds no person-review gate or fixed usage period. Separate deterministic citation resolution from semantic support, serious errors, uncertainty and actual reuse evidence. Review findings before TASK-001-06; fix and rerun failed gate conditions. Record one bounded repair and its reconsideration trigger; if it still fails the expected reader outcome or needs a product-direction change, bring the actual alternatives and evidence to the owner without labelling the experiment pass.

## Required deliverables

- Isolated two-project prototype and compatible draft Haeryong skill.
- Actual revision/claim/evidence worksheet, safe sample packages and reader walkthrough observations with participant type.
- G-VALUE record with separate structural and semantic results, limitations and next-contract decisions.
